# PROBLEM 3: ABSENCE OF CULTURALLY APPROPRIATE MENTAL HEALTH FIRST AID RESOURCES
## The Problem:
Mental health issues among adolescents and young adults, including anxiety, depression, trauma, and substance use, are rising. Yet young people across Uganda and the wider region face a compounded problem: not only do they lack access to culturally appropriate mental health services, they often cannot find or reach the services and resources that do exist.
The mental health resources currently available are largely Westernized, translated versions of clinical tools that do not reflect local expressions of distress, cultural norms around help-seeking, or the lived realities of youth in low-resource, post-conflict, or high-poverty contexts. But even setting cultural fit aside, the more immediate barrier is awareness and access. Young people, school counselors, youth workers, and community volunteers often do not know what support services exist, where they are, or how to reach them. People living with mental distress are left without a clear pathway to support.
## Who Is Affected:
People with mental distress across the general public, particularly adolescents and young adults.
## What Has Been Tried:
Mental health first aid resources available in the region are mostly translated versions of Western clinical tools that do not reflect local expressions of distress, cultural norms around help-seeking, or the lived realities of youth in post-conflict or poverty contexts. People also lack awareness and knowledge about existing mental health issues and how to seek support.
## Where Technology Could Help:
A tool that helps young people find and access mental health first aid resources and support services. Where locally grounded, culturally appropriate materials or databases already exist, the tool will surface and connect users to those. Where they do not, it will connect users to whatever credible support is available. The prototype could take the form of a digital resource hub, a service-finder platform, a peer support tool, or a combination.
